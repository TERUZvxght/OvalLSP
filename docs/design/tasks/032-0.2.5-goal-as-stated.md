# Task 032: 0.2.5's goal, as the maintainer stated it

`029` was drafted before this goal was given and does not carry it. This
file does. Where the two disagree about what 0.2.5 is for, **this one is
the source** and `029` is a set of candidate items produced under a
narrower reading.

> できた慣習や設計が根底に使われ、その上層に構築されているものに影響が

## What that says, and what it does not

**Says:** the subject is early-period conventions and designs that sit at
the foundation and affect everything built on them. Explicitly **not
only code** — three non-code instances are named by the maintainer
themselves: findings recorded and never harvested; version consistency
still maintained by hand; documents multiplying without order until size
is the problem. The register example is spelled out as organise → make
referenceable → define how entries are written from now on. Areas not yet
examined are to be swept and discussed rather than assumed fine.

**Does not say:** which items ship in 0.2.5, what the release boundary
is, or that anything must be restructured. "全体を綺麗に再設計" was
raised by the maintainer as a *question* about their own understanding
(「という認識で合っていますか?」) and was answered "half right" — the
project's own record of 0.1.12 (`024.15`: 47 files, four rounds, zero net
progress, rolled back) argues against a wholesale redesign, and `029`
rejects two specific centralisations on those grounds. Nothing here
overrides that.

**Hard boundary:** privacy and secret handling may not be simplified to
save effort. This is stated twice and is not a scoping input.

## Relationship to 029

`029` is code-design only: `M-1`–`M-3`, `S-1`–`S-6`, `C-1`–`C-3`. Its
`M-1` has shipped (on `feat/0.2.5`). Its remaining items are still
candidates and its evidence still stands, but **it is not the scope of
this release** — three of the four areas the maintainer named have no
item in it at all.

The eight-perspective audit and the four-way debate run on 2026-08-18
produced findings across all of them. Those findings are raw material;
the scope is not settled and is not settled by this file either. What
this file fixes is that the *goal* was recorded nowhere, so a later
session reading `029` would have taken a narrower release for the whole
instruction.

## The prioritisation this goal is subject to

From the same day, and from `docs/design/docs/01-product-requirements.md`
section 0: the top-level purpose is the LSP being genuinely usable at
1.0.0. Every item under this goal earns its place by serving that. The
maintainer's accuracy principle applies to the ordering — a wrong answer
is worse than no answer, **but letting 1.0.0 recede forever in pursuit of
accuracy is worse than either**, and a wrong answer is treated according
to how often the path is walked.

How that converts into "ship" or "defer" for a given item is **not** the
maintainer's ruling; it is a judgement each item has to argue. Section
0.4 of the requirements document offers one way of operationalising it,
marked there as this project's own working rule rather than theirs.
