# 0.3.4 — the arc that was opened as 0.4.0

**Branch:** `release/0.3.4`, merged into `main` by pull request. It began
life as `release/0.4.0`; every commit message on it says so, and none of
them is rewritten — the branch was renamed on 2026-09-06, when the
question "is there any capability in what shipped?" was asked and the
derived answer was no.

## Why the number changed

`docs/PUBLISHING.md`: a patch "makes the extension do what some earlier
release already claimed" and announces nothing new; a minor adds a
capability — a matrix row, a `NOT YET` turning ✅, a new setting or
command. The 23 entries closed on this branch are all `kind: defect`,
`docs/EXTENSION_CAPABILITIES.md` did not change by a byte, and neither
did `package.json` or `docs/ROADMAP.md`. The maintainer asked the
question; the register answered it.

Two facts made the rename cheap, and they are worth knowing next time:

- **Nothing shipped under the old number.** No tag, no Marketplace
  publish, no merge to `main`. The rename touched records, not
  publications.
- **The 0.4.0 queue was untouched.** All 23 closed entries carry numbers
  `024.323`–`024.345` — every one *created during this arc*. The 46 open
  entries routed to 0.4.0 during the 0.3.x closing sweeps were not worked
  at all, so nothing had to be re-targeted: 0.4.0 remains an unshipped
  future release that still owes exactly what `061` says it owes.

What could not be renamed: the commit messages, which say `0.4.0:`.
They are the immutable half of the record and this paragraph is the
pointer that resolves them.

## What is on the branch

Twenty-three defects, listed with dispositions in
`docs/reviews/2026-09-05-critical-review.md` (16 review findings, and the
table at its end), plus 3 regressions this branch's own fixes introduced
and 4 findings from the sweep that opened it. The changelog's 0.3.4
section carries the user-facing half; the register carries the reasoning.

The review method this branch settled into, recorded because it worked:
**two independent reviewers per milestone** — one via an external CLI,
one as a subagent — each told what a defect is and never where to look,
with every finding driven against the tree before anything was written
for it. The reviews found four defects the author's own suites could not:
three regressions and `024.345`, which is `024.338`'s shape in a second
layer. The register's `found by:` lines carry which review found what.

## Handover: what the next session must not lose

Written here because the maintainer plans a repository reorganisation
after 0.3.4, continuing from 0.3.5 — and a reorganisation is exactly when
loose ends fall off tables.

**Decisions that are still the maintainer's to make:**

1. **Tags `v0.3.2` and `v0.3.3` are not ancestors of `main`** — they were
   cut on release branches before the squash-merge, so `git describe
   main` answers `v0.3.1-...`. Recorded during the 0.3.x line; rewriting
   published refs is the maintainer's call, and 0.3.4's own tag placement
   should be decided *before* it is cut, not after.
2. **`diagnosticsMode` has three values and one is reachable** — `061`'s
   section stands unchanged: per-check severity (a 0.4.0 promise) either
   replaces that knob or must agree with it, and the decision precedes
   the first line of that feature.
3. **The 0.4.0 promises themselves** — per-check severity,
   auto-`require`, signature-help `activeParameter`. Untouched, still
   publicly promised in `docs/ROADMAP.md`, both READMEs and the site.
   `061` records their prerequisites; `activeParameter` is the only one
   whose inputs already exist.

**Review threads that are open, with their state:**

4. **`024.45` is measured and not fixed.** Save-to-publish is 4.22 s and
   11.25 s; a hover during it waits 4.33 s and 12.79 s, against a stated
   300 ms. The profile in the entry says where the time is. This is the
   one finding of the 2026-09-05 review (R15) still open.
5. **The follow-up review's "Partly met" column is honest and unpaid.**
   Fifteen fixes pass their own examples while the review's 「確認する
   条件」 asked for wider controls (`docs/reviews/2026-09-06-astra-followup.md`
   — read its sections 4 and 5 before trusting its table). Whoever next
   touches one of those areas should read that finding's condition first.
6. **Planned review lenses that never ran.** A ten-lens round was
   designed and stopped for rate limits after one lens completed (it
   found `024.345`; a later single run of the memo-staleness lens closed
   the census in `78ac99c`). The lenses still unrun, in the order they
   were judged likely to pay: rename-safety's under-refusal side
   (`prepend`, `alias_method`, `method_missing`, applying returned edits
   and running them); vacuous-specs (copy changed specs onto the parent
   library — every non-control example should fail there; this arc
   produced five such examples, all found by reviewers); the guard/macro
   lens; containment; publish-ordering re-orderings; the extension
   lifecycle; and a claims re-derivation. The prompts lived outside the
   repository and are gone; the lens names above are their substance.
7. **The Astra follow-up's prepared-but-unrun probes are lost** — they
   lived in `/private/tmp/astra-followup/` and were never executed nor
   counted as evidence. Nothing to recover; recorded so nobody goes
   looking.

**Small open items already in `docs/ISSUES.md` intake** (the register's
front door — none of these needs this document to survive, listed so the
reorganisation counts them): the check-tooling quartet from `059`; the

One comment the reviews caught as false — `#publish_findings`' disk
