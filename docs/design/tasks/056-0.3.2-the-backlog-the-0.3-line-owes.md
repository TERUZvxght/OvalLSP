# 056 — 0.3.2: the backlog the 0.3 line owes

**Branch:** `release/0.3.2`, merging into `main` by pull request — and
`release/0.3.2-published`, which carries the two publishing commits made
after that merge (`a33d17a`, `e7781d6`). `main` squash-merged them as
`531362c`, so they are reachable only from that branch, which is why
`CONTRIBUTING.md`'s "A merged release branch is kept" covers it too. <!-- deleted -->
`CLAUDE.md`'s "Where a release's work lives" has the rule, and
`scripts/check_release_pointers.rb` fails when a release branch exists
that no task document names — which is what this line is. **It would not
have failed for the second branch.** Its `RELEASE_BRANCH` pattern matches
`release/<major>.<minor>.<patch>` and nothing else, so a suffixed name is
dropped from `visible_branches` before the naming test is applied: the
check reported three release branches while four existed. This line is
what names it; widening the pattern is the script's own change to make.

**Scope:** the thirty-four register entries targeted at 0.3.2 when the
release opened. That is a release's worth of work by any measure, and
this document records what each of them turned out to be rather than
assuming the target was right. The 0.3.1 record is the reason for that
caution: both of *its* entries were wrong about the thing they
asserted, and driving them before working on them is what found it.

**A patch moves no capability row** (`docs/PUBLISHING.md`). Several of
the thirty-four turn a silence into an answer, which that document puts
in a minor. Where driving one shows it cannot be contained in a patch,
it is retargeted here with the measurement that says so — not moved
quietly, and not forced into the release to keep a number.

## What was done

Thirty-four owed, and every one of them driven. **Nineteen fixed;
fifteen moved to 0.4.0 with the measurement that says why a patch cannot
hold them.** Nothing was reassigned without being run first, and nothing
was closed without it either — which is the caution this document opened
with and the reason three of the **thirty-four** came out differently
from their own records. Two of those three are among the nineteen fixed
rather than the fifteen moved; this sentence said "three of the fifteen"
until the arithmetic was checked against the tables below it.

**Three of the thirty-four were records that did not survive being
driven.** `024.137`'s premise was false — `#search` has not scanned every
symbol since 0.2.16, which the published limitation already said and the
entry did not. `024.316` recorded two guards as redundant on a
measurement that could not reach either of them. `024.275` had had three
releases to reproduce and had not. That is the argument for driving
before working rather than a complaint about the register: each was
found by running it, and none by reading it.

the fifteen above, each with the measurement rather than a note.
